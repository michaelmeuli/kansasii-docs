# Mkan329-054 (TNR 2023500007): ethionamide resistance without an explaining mutation

Checked 2026-11-18. Species *M. persicum* (`screening_map_results.csv`).

## Summary

**Mkan329-054 has an ethionamide (ETH) MIC of 10 mg/l. No mutation in ethA, the other known ETH resistance genes, or anywhere
else in the genome explains it.** 054 is the only isolate in the cohort with ETH > 1.2 that is used in kansasii-mic.

- **ethA:** the protein is identical in all 30 persicum isolates. 054 has two silent changes and one change 4 bp before the start codon; Mkan329-009 (ETH 0.6) has the same three.
- **Other ETH genes** (ethR, inhA plus promoter, fabG1, mshA, ndh, katG): every change in 054 is also found in susceptible isolates, except mshA H463R. That arginine is the residue *M. tuberculosis* has (R454), so it is unlikely to switch the enzyme off.
- **Genome-wide:** 054 has no close relative in the cohort. Compared with 27 other persicum assemblies, it has 1,069 unique sites and 36 unique genes. None is in a known ETH activation, target or mycothiol gene. The only efflux-type lead is weak: TetR regulator OALCOA_01729 (G71E), which sits between two MmpL transporter genes.
- **Recommendation:** retest the ETH MIC on a fresh subculture before doing more genomics. If 054 is resistant again, look at the TetR/MmpL locus first.

## Locating ethA in the kansasii complex

ethA is not named in the ATCC 12478 GFF, which lists about 17 Baeyer-Villiger monooxygenases. I found it by searching
with the *M. tuberculosis* EthA protein (Rv3854c, tblastn):

| Genome | ethA | ethR | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| ATCC 12478 (GCF_000157895.3) | MKAN_RS13735, NC_022663 3143947-3145416 (−), 87 % aa to Rv3854c | MKAN_RS13740, 3145493-3146140 (+), 83 % to Rv3855 | divergent ethA/ethR pair as in Mtb; MKAN_RS21445 (74 %) is a paralog. Both are targets in kansasii-amr-marker-screen since commit `1fe1685` |
| Mkan329-054 (Bakta) | OALCOA_00155 (87 %, 489 aa) | | Bakta labels **OALCOA_00005** "ethA", but it is a paralog with only 51 % identity |

Every persicum genome has one full-length ethA ortholog (489 aa). mymA (Rv3083), the alternative ETH activator, has no
ortholog in persicum; the best hits are the ethA paralogs at 49 %.

## Genome-wide comparison

**054 vs 009** (snippy 4.6.0, reads of each isolate on the other's assembly):

- SNPs: 8,632 differences. Calling 009's own reads against its assembly removed 6 of them as 009 assembly errors.
- Gene content: about 310 kb of 009 is missing from 054, and about 100 kb of 054 is missing from 009. Most of it is phages, transposons and plasmid-like elements.
- 009 is therefore not a near-clonal control.

**054 vs the 27 other persicum assemblies** (snippy `--ctgs` on the 054 assembly, then snippy-core):

- **1,069 sites where 054 differs from every called isolate** (at least 20 of 27 called):
  - 477 silent, 386 change the protein, 113 between genes, and 89 secondary positions of multi-base variants;
  - 4 change a start or stop codon, in PPE38, an FHA-domain protein, a hypothetical protein and mobA. None of these genes is linked to ETH.
  - 211 of the sites (125 silent, 40 missense) fall in one 9.3 kb multi-module polyketide synthase, OALCOA_02062. Bakta labels it "fabY", but it is not KasA/KasB. A block this dense looks like recombination, not selection.
- **36 CDS found only in 054** (blastn ≥80 % identity, ≥50 % coverage): all in one prophage and two plasmid- or integrase-like islands. None is a monooxygenase, transporter or resistance gene.

## Mkan329-186 (TNR 2020500010)

- Also *M. persicum* with ETH 10, but from `screening_map_ast_jc.csv`.
- kansasii-mic drops it on purpose: it is in `MANUAL_EXCLUSIONS` in `loading.py` because `kansasii_list_JC.xlsx` marks it "nicht rein" (not pure). See EXCLUSIONS.md.
- Its ethA, ethR, inhA plus promoter, fabG1, mshA, ndh and katG are identical to the persicum consensus, protein and upstream.
- Read check: at least 40× depth over the seven genes; no minority variant above 9 % (only 2-3 reads each, typical sequencing errors).
- Its ETH 10 most likely reflects the impure culture.

## Caveats

- snippy labels effects from 054 to the others, so they read mirrored. For example, "stop_gained" in PPE38 means the
  *others* have the stop codon and 054 does not.
- In `scratch/eth_persicum/out/hits.tsv`, coordinates of minus-strand hits are on the reverse-complement strand. The
  extracted sequences are correct; use the table above for ATCC 12478 coordinates.
- `core.tab` holds SNPs and short complex variants only. Indels unique to 054 are not screened genome-wide. They are
  covered for the seven ETH genes by the per-gene comparison and the read check.
- Phenotype from a single measurement each.
